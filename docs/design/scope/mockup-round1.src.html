Big Watts: scope

Interval Screen's big-watts styles as their own app. Colours follow the Karoo's power zones (rider profile → FTP / power zones), so no target, no workouts, no intervals.icu.

1 · What Interval Screen has today

Tap the watts to cycle through five styles. Styles 2–5 colour against a target range from an intervals.icu workout. Without a workout you get the free-ride screen (plain watts, Lap avg, HR zone tile, laps).

2 · Big Watts: one data field, the watts big in the middle

3 s power, as big as the field allows, with its Karoo zone above it. Tap the watts to cycle the five styles (remembered). Colours are the zone the watts are in. Put it on a page alone or next to Karoo's own fields.
